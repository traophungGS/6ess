Rules of '6ess'

Board & Setup

6x6 Board. Bottom-right square (f1) is dark. White pieces on rank 1 & 2; Black pieces on rank 5 & 6.

Rank 1 (White): a1:Rook, b1:Bishop, c1:Queen, d1:King, e1:Bishop, f1:Knight.
Rank 6 (Black): a6:Rook, b6:Bishop, c6:Queen, d6:King, e6:Bishop, f6:Knight.

Pawns & Promotion

  • Pawns move 1 square forward only (No 2-square step, No En Passant).
  • Pawns promote only upon reaching the back rank (Rank 6 for White, Rank 1 for Black).
  • Mandatory Promotion: Pawns can ONLY be promoted to pieces of your own color that have ALREADY BEEN CAPTURED.
  • Dummy / Frozen Pawns: If NO captured pieces are available when reaching the end, the pawn is NOT promoted and becomes a frozen pawn. It cannot be moved by you, but can still be captured by opponent.

Castling Rules

Castling is ONLY possible on the Queen's side (with the Rook on file 'a').

  • King moves 2 squares left ($d1 \to b1$), Rook moves past $a1 \to c1$.
  • Notation used is 0-0.
  • Requires no previous move by King/Rook, clear squares between them, and King cannot move through or into check.
